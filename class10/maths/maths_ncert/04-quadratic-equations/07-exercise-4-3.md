## EXERCISE 4.3

1. Find the nature of the roots of the following quadratic equations. If the real roots exist, find them :
   - (i) $2x^2 - 3x + 5 = 0$
   - (ii) $3x^2 - 4\sqrt{3}\, x + 4 = 0$
   - (iii) $2x^2 - 6x + 3 = 0$

2. Find the values of $k$ for each of the following quadratic equations, so that they have two equal roots.
   - (i) $2x^2 + kx + 3 = 0$
   - (ii) $kx(x - 2) + 6 = 0$

3. Is it possible to design a rectangular mango grove whose length is twice its breadth, and the area is 800 m²? If so, find its length and breadth.

4. Is the following situation possible? If so, determine their present ages. The sum of the ages of two friends is 20 years. Four years ago, the product of their ages in years was 48.

5. Is it possible to design a rectangular park of perimeter 80 m and area 400 m²? If so, find its length and breadth.

---

## Solutions

**1.** Nature of roots (use discriminant $D = b^2 - 4ac$) and roots if real :

- (i) $2x^2 - 3x + 5 = 0$: $a=2$, $b=-3$, $c=5$. $D = 9 - 40 = -31 < 0$. No real roots.
- (ii) $3x^2 - 4\sqrt{3}\, x + 4 = 0$: $D = 48 - 48 = 0$. Two equal real roots. $x = \dfrac{4\sqrt{3}}{6} = \dfrac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}$.
- (iii) $2x^2 - 6x + 3 = 0$: $D = 36 - 24 = 12 > 0$. Two distinct real roots. $x = \dfrac{6 \pm \sqrt{12}}{4} = \dfrac{3 \pm \sqrt{3}}{2}$.

**2.** Two equal roots $\Rightarrow$ discriminant $= 0$.

- (i) $2x^2 + kx + 3 = 0$: $D = k^2 - 24 = 0$ $\Rightarrow$ $k = \pm 2\sqrt{6}$.
- (ii) $kx(x-2) + 6 = 0$ $\Rightarrow$ $kx^2 - 2kx + 6 = 0$. $D = 4k^2 - 24k = 0$ $\Rightarrow$ $4k(k-6) = 0$. So $k = 0$ or $k = 6$. For quadratic, $k \neq 0$. Hence $k = 6$.

**3.** Let breadth = $x$ m, length = $2x$ m. Area $= 2x^2 = 800$ $\Rightarrow$ $x^2 = 400$ $\Rightarrow$ $x = 20$. Yes, possible. Length = 40 m, breadth = 20 m.

**4.** Let present ages be $x$ and $(20-x)$ years. Four years ago: $(x-4)(16-x) = 48$ $\Rightarrow$ $-x^2 + 20x - 64 = 48$ $\Rightarrow$ $x^2 - 20x + 112 = 0$. $D = 400 - 448 = -48 < 0$. No real roots. So the situation is not possible.

**5.** Let length = $x$ m, breadth = $(40-x)$ m (since $2(x + \text{breadth}) = 80$). Area $= x(40-x) = 400$ $\Rightarrow$ $x^2 - 40x + 400 = 0$ $\Rightarrow$ $(x-20)^2 = 0$. So $x = 20$. Yes, possible. Length = breadth = 20 m (square park).
