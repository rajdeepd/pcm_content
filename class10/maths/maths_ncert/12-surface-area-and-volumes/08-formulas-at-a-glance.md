## Formulas at a glance (Chapter 12)

**Notation:** radius $r$, height $h$, slant height $l$, length $L$, breadth $B$, edge $a$.

**Quick rules (combination of solids):**

- **Volume** of the combined solid $=$ **sum of volumes** of the parts.
- **Surface area to paint/cover** $=$ **sum of only the exposed surface areas** (do not count the joined faces).

### Basic solids

| Solid | Curved/Lateral surface area | Total surface area | Volume |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| **Cuboid** ($L \times B \times h$) | $2h(L+B)$ | $2(LB+Bh+hL)$ | $LBh$ |
| **Cube** (edge $a$) | $4a^2$ | $6a^2$ | $a^3$ |
| **Cylinder** (radius $r$, height $h$) | $2\pi rh$ | $2\pi r(h+r)$ | $\pi r^2h$ |
| **Cone** (radius $r$, height $h$) | $\pi rl$ | $\pi r(l+r)$ | $\dfrac{1}{3}\pi r^2h$ |
| **Sphere** (radius $r$) | $4\pi r^2$ | $4\pi r^2$ | $\dfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3$ |
| **Hemisphere** (radius $r$) | $2\pi r^2$ | $3\pi r^2$ | $\dfrac{2}{3}\pi r^3$ |

### Useful relations

- **Slant height of cone:** $l=\sqrt{r^2+h^2}$
- **Area of circle:** $\pi r^2$
- **Circumference of circle:** $2\pi r$

**Common $\pi$ values:** $\pi \approx \dfrac{22}{7}$ or $\pi \approx 3.14$ (use the one given in the question).
