<h4 style="text-transform: none;">6. Assertion (A): Diamond does not conduct electricity.</h4>

**Reason (R):** Diamond has high refractive index.

---

<h4 style="text-transform: none;">7. Assertion (A): Alcohol can not be used as an additive in petrol.</h4>

**Reason (R):** Alcohol give rise to $CO_2$ and $H_2O$ when burnt in sufficient air.

---

<h4 style="text-transform: none;">8. Assertion (A): In a candle, wax vapours burn in sufficient supply of oxygen, which leads to blue flame.</h4>

**Reason (R):** When the oxygen supply is sufficient, then fuels burn completely producing a blue flame.

## Case Based Questions

Direction [Q.Nos. (i)-(iii)] Answer the questions on the basis of your understanding of the following passage and related studied concepts:

<h4 style="text-transform: none;">9. The table shows some information about compounds in a homologous series.</h4>

| Name of compound | Molecular formula | Relative molecular mass | Boiling point/°C |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Methanol | $CH_3OH$ | 32 | 65 |
| Ethanol | $C_2H_5OH$ | 46 | 78 |
| Propan-1-ol | $C_3H_7OH$ | 60 | 97 |
| Butan-1-ol | $C_4H_9OH$ | 74 | 117 |
| Pentan-1-ol | $C_5H_{11}OH$ | 88 | 138 |

A lot of ethyl alcohol (ethanol) is used in industry for manufacturing various products. For industrial purposes, ethyl alcohol is supplied ‘duty free’ (without charging production tax) by the government. This makes the industrial alcohol much cheaper than its market rate. To prevent the misuse of industrial purposes it is denatured by adding small amount of poisonous substances. The addition of small amount of $CuSO_4$ imparts a blue colour to industrial ethyl alcohol so that it can be identified easily.

(i) Predict the relative molecular mass of the compound, in the same series, which has seven carbon atoms in one molecule.

(ii) Write the general formula for a compound in this homologous series.

(iii) (a) State the two uses of ethanol.  
(b) Write the two effects of ethanol on living beings.

**Or**

(iii) What do you mean by homologous series? Write its two characteristics.
