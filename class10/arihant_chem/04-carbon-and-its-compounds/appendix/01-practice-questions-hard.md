## Assertion–Reason Questions (Hard)

<div class="intro-box" style="background-color: #fff3cd; border-left-color: #ffc107;">
<strong>Direction (A–R):</strong> Mark the correct option:<br/>
(a) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.<br/>
(b) Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A.<br/>
(c) A is true, but R is false.<br/>
(d) A is false, but R is true.
</div>

<h4 style="text-transform: none;">AR1. Assertion (A): Ethanol does not decolourise bromine water.</h4>
**Reason (R):** Ethanol is a saturated compound and generally does not undergo addition reactions.

---

<h4 style="text-transform: none;">AR2. Assertion (A): Soap solutions are basic in nature.</h4>
**Reason (R):** Soaps are salts of strong acid and weak base.

---

<h4 style="text-transform: none;">AR3. Assertion (A): Propanone cannot be oxidised easily under mild oxidising conditions.</h4>
**Reason (R):** Ketones are generally resistant to oxidation compared to aldehydes.

---

<h4 style="text-transform: none;">AR4. Assertion (A): Ethanoic acid turns blue litmus red.</h4>
**Reason (R):** Ethanoic acid ionises completely in aqueous solution.

---

<h4 style="text-transform: none;">AR5. Assertion (A): Unsaturated hydrocarbons show addition reactions.</h4>
**Reason (R):** \(\pi\)-bond in unsaturated hydrocarbons is weaker and breaks to form new \(\sigma\)-bonds.
