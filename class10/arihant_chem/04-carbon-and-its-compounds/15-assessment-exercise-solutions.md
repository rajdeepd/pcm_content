## MCQ Solutions

<h4 style="text-transform: none;">1. Consider the following structure of ethyl propanoate.</h4>

Structure: $CH_3-CH_2-C(=O)-O-CH_2-CH_3$

<p class="answer-text"><strong>Answer:</strong> (c)</p>

<p class="answer-text"><strong class="sol-label">Sol.</strong> The number of bonding pairs equals the total number of bonds (a double bond has 2 bonding pairs: one \(\sigma\) and one \(\pi\)). In $CH_3-CH_2-C(=O)-O-CH_2-CH_3$:</p>

<p class="answer-text" style="margin-top: -10px;">\(C-H\) bonds \(= 10\); single bonds between heavy atoms \(= 5\); and the \(C=O\) double bond contributes 2 bonding pairs. Total bonding pairs \(= 10 + 5 + 2 = 17\).</p>

## Assertion-Reason Solutions

<h4 style="text-transform: none;">6. Assertion (A): Diamond does not conduct electricity.</h4>

**Reason (R):** Diamond has high refractive index.

<p class="answer-text"><strong>Answer:</strong> (b)</p>

<p class="answer-text"><strong class="sol-label">Sol.</strong> Assertion is true: diamond does not conduct electricity (no free electrons). Reason is also true (diamond has a high refractive index) but it does not explain non-conductivity. Hence (b).</p>

---

<h4 style="text-transform: none;">7. Assertion (A): Alcohol can not be used as an additive in petrol.</h4>

**Reason (R):** Alcohol give rise to $CO_2$ and $H_2O$ when burnt in sufficient air.

<p class="answer-text"><strong>Answer:</strong> (d)</p>

<p class="answer-text"><strong class="sol-label">Sol.</strong> Alcohol (ethanol) <em>can</em> be used as an additive in petrol (e.g., ethanol blending), so Assertion is false. On complete combustion, alcohol produces $CO_2$ and $H_2O$, so Reason is true. Hence (d).</p>

---

<h4 style="text-transform: none;">8. Assertion (A): In a candle, wax vapours burn in sufficient supply of oxygen, which leads to blue flame.</h4>

**Reason (R):** When the oxygen supply is sufficient, then fuels burn completely producing a blue flame.

<p class="answer-text"><strong>Answer:</strong> (a)</p>

<p class="answer-text"><strong class="sol-label">Sol.</strong> In sufficient oxygen, wax vapours undergo complete combustion giving a blue (non-luminous) flame. Thus both Assertion and Reason are true and Reason correctly explains Assertion. Hence (a).</p>

## Case Based Question Solutions

<h4 style="text-transform: none;">9. The table shows some information about compounds in a homologous series.</h4>

| Name of compound | Molecular formula | Relative molecular mass | Boiling point/°C |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Methanol | $CH_3OH$ | 32 | 65 |
| Ethanol | $C_2H_5OH$ | 46 | 78 |
| Propan-1-ol | $C_3H_7OH$ | 60 | 97 |
| Butan-1-ol | $C_4H_9OH$ | 74 | 117 |
| Pentan-1-ol | $C_5H_{11}OH$ | 88 | 138 |

A lot of ethyl alcohol (ethanol) is used in industry for manufacturing various products. For industrial purposes, ethyl alcohol is supplied ‘duty free’ (without charging production tax) by the government. This makes the industrial alcohol much cheaper than its market rate. To prevent the misuse of industrial purposes it is denatured by adding small amount of poisonous substances. The addition of small amount of $CuSO_4$ imparts a blue colour to industrial ethyl alcohol so that it can be identified easily.

<h4 style="text-transform: none;">9 (i)</h4>

<p class="answer-text"><strong class="sol-label">Sol.</strong> In this homologous series of alcohols, each successive member differs by \(CH_2\) (mass 14). For 7 carbon atoms: \(C_7H_{15}OH\) \((= C_7H_{16}O)\).</p>

Relative molecular mass \(= 7\times 12 + 16\times 1 + 16 = 84 + 16 + 16 = 116\).

<h4 style="text-transform: none;">9 (ii)</h4>

<p class="answer-text"><strong class="sol-label">Sol.</strong> General formula of alkanols (alcohols): \(C_nH_{2n+1}OH\) (or \(C_nH_{2n+2}O\)).</p>

<h4 style="text-transform: none;">9 (iii)(a)</h4>

<p class="answer-text"><strong class="sol-label">Sol.</strong> Two uses of ethanol: (i) as a solvent (medicines/perfumes/varnishes), (ii) as a fuel/additive in petrol (spirit, gasohol) or in sanitizers/antiseptics.</p>

<h4 style="text-transform: none;">9 (iii)(b)</h4>

<p class="answer-text"><strong class="sol-label">Sol.</strong> Two effects of ethanol on living beings: (i) depresses the central nervous system causing impaired judgement/coordination, (ii) long-term use can cause addiction and liver damage.</p>

<h4 style="text-transform: none;">9 (iii) Or</h4>

<p class="answer-text"><strong class="sol-label">Sol.</strong> A homologous series is a family of organic compounds with the same functional group and similar chemical properties, where successive members differ by \(CH_2\).</p>

Characteristics (any two):
<br/>- Same functional group and same general formula.
<br/>- Successive members differ by \(CH_2\) and show a gradual change in physical properties.

<h4 style="text-transform: none;">11. NaCl conducts electricity in molten state but not in solid state whereas hydrocarbons are non-conductors of electricity in solid, molten or aqueous state.</h4>

<span class="badge-competency">Competency Based Que.</span>

<h4 style="text-transform: none;">11 (i)</h4>

<p class="answer-text"><strong class="sol-label">Sol.</strong> NaCl conducts in molten state because ions are free to move; in solid state ions are fixed in the lattice. Hydrocarbons are covalent and do not form ions, hence they do not conduct in any state.</p>

<h4 style="text-transform: none;">11 (ii)</h4>

<p class="answer-text"><strong class="sol-label">Sol.</strong> Ionic compounds (like NaCl) are generally soluble in water, while hydrocarbons are insoluble in water but soluble in organic solvents.</p>

<h4 style="text-transform: none;">12. (i) Name the allotropes of carbon. Why is diamond called the hardest substance?</h4>

<h4 style="text-transform: none;">12 (i)</h4>

<p class="answer-text"><strong class="sol-label">Sol.</strong> Allotropes of carbon: diamond, graphite (also fullerenes). Diamond is the hardest due to a rigid 3D network of strong covalent bonds.</p>

<h4 style="text-transform: none;">12. (ii) Describe the structure of graphite.</h4>

<h4 style="text-transform: none;">12 (ii)</h4>

<p class="answer-text"><strong class="sol-label">Sol.</strong> Graphite has a layered structure of hexagonal rings. Each carbon is bonded to three others; layers are held by weak forces and can slide, so graphite is soft and conducts electricity.</p>

<h4 style="text-transform: none;">15. (i) State the reason, why carbon can neither form $C^{4+}$ nor $C^{4-}$ anions but forms covalent compounds.</h4>

<span class="badge-competency">Competency Based Que.</span>

<h4 style="text-transform: none;">15. (ii) How many covalent bonds are there in a molecule of ethane?</h4>

<h4 style="text-transform: none;">15. (iii) We can wash our clothes even in hard water by using detergent. Give reason.</h4>

<h4 style="text-transform: none;">15 (i)</h4>

<p class="answer-text"><strong class="sol-label">Sol.</strong> Formation of \(C^{4+}\) requires removal of four electrons (very high ionisation energy). Formation of \(C^{4-}\) is unstable due to strong repulsion on adding four electrons. Hence carbon forms covalent bonds by sharing electrons.</p>

<h4 style="text-transform: none;">15 (ii)</h4>

<p class="answer-text"><strong class="sol-label">Sol.</strong> Ethane \(C_2H_6\) has 6 \(C-H\) bonds and 1 \(C-C\) bond, total 7 covalent bonds.</p>

<h4 style="text-transform: none;">15 (iii)</h4>

<p class="answer-text"><strong class="sol-label">Sol.</strong> Detergents do not form insoluble scum with \(Ca^{2+}\)/\(Mg^{2+}\) ions of hard water; they form soluble salts, so they clean effectively.</p>

<h4 style="text-transform: none;">21. An organic compound A with molecular formula $C_4H_8O_2$ on alkaline hydrolysis gives two compounds B and C. On acidification with dil. HCl, it gives D. Oxidation of C with $K_2Cr_2O_7/H_2SO_4$ also gives D. Identify A, B, C and D and explain all the reactions involved.</h4>

<h4 style="text-transform: none;">21</h4>

<p class="answer-text"><strong class="sol-label">Sol.</strong> A is an ester (\(C_4H_8O_2\)) that on alkaline hydrolysis gives a salt (B) and an alcohol (C). Acidification of B gives carboxylic acid D. Oxidation of C also gives D. Hence:</p>

- \(A\): ethyl ethanoate, $CH_3COOCH_2CH_3$
- \(B\): sodium ethanoate, $CH_3COONa$
- \(C\): ethanol, $C_2H_5OH$
- \(D\): ethanoic acid, $CH_3COOH$

Reactions:

$$CH_3COOCH_2CH_3 + NaOH \rightarrow CH_3COONa + C_2H_5OH$$

$$CH_3COONa + HCl \rightarrow CH_3COOH + NaCl$$

$$C_2H_5OH \xrightarrow{K_2Cr_2O_7/H_2SO_4} CH_3COOH$$
