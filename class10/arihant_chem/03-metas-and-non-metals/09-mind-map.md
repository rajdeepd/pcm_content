# Mind Map - Metals and Non-metals

## Key Concepts at a Glance

### Physical Properties
- **Metals:** Lustrous, hard, malleable, ductile, good conductors, sonorous, high m.p./b.p.
- **Non-metals:** Dull, soft, brittle, poor conductors, non-sonorous, low m.p./b.p.
- **Exceptions:** Mercury (liquid metal), Iodine (lustrous non-metal), Diamond (hardest non-metal), Graphite (conducting non-metal)

### Chemical Properties of Metals
- Reaction with O₂ → Metal oxides (basic/amphoteric)
- Reaction with H₂O → Metal hydroxide + H₂
- Reaction with acids → Salt + H₂
- Displacement reactions → More reactive displaces less reactive

### Reactivity Series
K > Na > Ca > Mg > Al > Zn > Fe > Pb > [H] > Cu > Hg > Ag > Au

### Ionic Compounds
- Formed by transfer of electrons (metal → non-metal)
- High m.p./b.p., soluble in water, conduct electricity in molten/aqueous state

### Extraction of Metals
- Low reactivity → Heating alone
- Medium reactivity → Roasting/Calcination + Reduction with carbon
- High reactivity → Electrolytic reduction

### Corrosion & Prevention
- Rusting = Fe + O₂ + H₂O → Hydrated iron oxide
- Prevention: Painting, oiling, galvanisation, electroplating, alloying
